Machine learning services

Our services focus exclusively on machine learning, including deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, predictive modelling and model evaluation.

What does implementation mean? It means writing and running the research code: preparing your dataset, building the algorithm or model, conducting experiments and producing evaluation results.

PACKAGE A

Coding & experiments

You provide: Your machine learning research idea, proposed method and usable dataset.

We provide: Data preprocessing, machine learning model code, baseline comparisons, experiments, evaluation tables and graphs, and a code handover.

Choose this when you need technical implementation. Manuscript drafting is not included.

PACKAGE B

Manuscript development

You provide: Your completed research, methodology, verified results and references.

We provide: Help drafting and editing the paper, organising tables and figures, formatting references and preparing the venue template.

Choose this when your experiments are complete and you need help presenting them in a paper.

PACKAGE C

Research planning & manuscript

You provide: Your topic and available data. You carry out the experiments and supply verified results.

We provide: Help refining the research question, method and experiment plan, followed by manuscript development once your results are ready.

Choose this when you can perform the coding yourself but need research direction and manuscript support.

PACKAGE D

Planning, coding & manuscript

You provide: Your research area, objectives, available data and feedback throughout the project.

We provide: Collaborative research planning, code development, experiments, analysis and manuscript preparation.

Choose this for support across all three stages. You review and validate the research and final claims.
